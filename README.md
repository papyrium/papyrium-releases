This repository is used for distributing official Papyrium builds and tracking issues.

It does NOT contain application source code.

## About Papyrium

Papyrium is a desktop app for building and managing your personal digital book library — designed for readers who want more than a folder of files.
### Library management
- Internal library — a built-in managed folder with an Inbox for dropping in new books
- Works with your existing folders — connect a local folder or NAS share and Papyrium indexes it in place. Your files stay where they are, your folder structure is preserved.
- Automatic metadata extraction from PDF and EPUB files
- Organize with tags, categories, collections, and series
- Smart collections that auto-populate based on reading status, genre, date added, and more
- Search with filters
### Built-in reader
- Read files without leaving the app
- Text selection, highlights, and freeform drawing annotations
- Reading progress tracking
### Reading workflow
- Track what you're reading, finished, and planning to read
- "Currently Reading" and "Recently Added" smart collections out of the box
- Per-book notes panel

AI features are on the way.
